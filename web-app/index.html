How It Works

Scrambling: Divides your image into 64 tiles (8×8 grid), shuffles them deterministically, and adds a marker column with encoding information.

Unscrambling: Automatically detects the marker column, extracts the seed, and reconstructs the original image. No keys needed!

Note: This is visual obfuscation, not encryption. It increases the cost of bulk AI scraping but won't stop determined attackers.
